lidar-game

Alone in the dark with a scanner. What you scan stays.

Controls
  • Trigger (scanner hand): scan. Squeeze harder to narrow the beam.
  • Left stick: walk. Right stick: turn.
  • Look at your free palm for the menu. Point the scanner at it and pull the trigger.
Running this on a Steam Frame
  • Stream from this PC: start SteamVR, open this page in Chrome or Edge on the desktop, and press Enter VR.
  • No headset? Add ?emulate to the URL for an emulated one.
